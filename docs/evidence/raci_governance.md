## 2. Six-Sprint Meeting & Checkpoint Cadence
| Ceremony | Frequency | Duration | Participants | Expected Output / Evidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sprint Planning** | Start of each Sprint | 30 min | Full PCM Team | Committed Sprint Backlog & Sprint Goal in `docs/`. |
| **Daily Scrum** | Daily (before 20:00) | 10 min | Full PCM Team | Updates logged in `docs/dailies.md` & `sprint_log.csv`. |
| **Sprint Review** | End of each Sprint | 15 min | PCM Team + Sponsor | Live demo of DoD-compliant stories & feedback minutes. |
| **Sprint Retrospective** | End of each Sprint | 15 min | Full PCM Team | Start/Stop/Continue action items in `docs/retro_sprintX.md`. |

## 3. Escalation & Decision Flow
1. **Level 1 (Technical / Data Impediments - e.g., R-01):** Logged immediately in Trello `Blocked/Risks` list by the story owner and addressed in the next Daily Scrum via Pair Programming.
2. **Level 2 (Scope / Schedule Drift - R-02, R-03):** Escalated by the Scrum Facilitator to the Project Leader within 24 hours; mitigated via Sprint Backlog recalibration.
3. **Level 3 (Strategic / Compliance Impact - R-04):** Escalated by the Project Leader to the FinBank Sponsor at the Sprint Review checkpoint.
